HE WAS NEVER GONE.

For ten years every Minecraft update said Removed Herobrine. This mod puts him back — as the last boss Minecraft never shipped. Something goes wrong in your world. A trail leads to a door. Behind it is his dimension, and him. End him, and it's over. For real.

Download latest · .jar · Minecraft 26.2 · Fabric

Server modpack (.mrpack) · How to install

  1. 1Small things go wrong. A door opens on its own. Leaves fall off a whole wood at once. A villager follows you with white eyes.
  2. 2A man named Addexio finds you, and walks you house to house through what he did.
  3. 3The last house has a door into his world. It shuts behind you. Bring friends.
