HE IS NEVERSEEN ARRIVING

A survival horror mod for Minecraft 26.2. Six phases, and the first five are deniable — a sound, a snuffed torch, a shape at the treeline you could talk yourself out of. Then it stops being deniable.

Download latest release How to install

Fabric · Minecraft 26.2 · Java 25+ · single-player & servers

Most horror mods answer the question. This one refuses to — for hours. Every early event has a mundane explanation available, and the fear lives in the gap between something happened and I can prove it.

Six phases

Wrath is one number per world. It only ever goes up — and the things you do to feel safer are the things that raise it.

  1. Rumour

    Footsteps behind you, once, with nothing there. A torch you placed, on the ground, unlit. A heartbeat buried in rock, with nothing behind it.

  2. Watcher

    He is simply there, at distance, and gone when you look. Whoever spots him first spends the clock — so your friend saw him and you didn't.

  3. Trespasser

    Four words on your wall, written from your own statistics. Pages of somebody else's account, on the floor where you will find them.

  4. Mimic

    One of your animals stops being an animal. It follows. It waits when you turn.

  5. Hunter

    He follows — faster than your sprint. Shelter stops working: he opens what opens and mines through what doesn't. The day ends early and every torch you own goes out at once.

  6. Siege

    The night does not end. The storm does not stop. Every animal in the field turns, and every one of them is looking at you. And for the first time in forty hours, he can be hurt.

What's in it

A town that isn't on any map

Walled, populated, eight houses and a hall and a forge with its front wall missing. Under the square, a vaulted chamber with streets, lantern posts and a library holding more books than the whole town above it. Two ways down — and neither is a lever.

Five houses, in order

Each one less like somewhere a person lived than the last. The first has the furniture still in it. The last is not a house.

Nothing irreversible

Torches drop. Blocks he mines drop. Nothing he does costs you an item — right up until the ending, which is allowed to leave a mark, and which you can switch off.

An ending you start

Thirty blows, counted rather than measured, so a netherite axe changes how it looks and never how long it takes. At the tenth, a church goes up with your grave outside it.

Built for company

He works through a group one at a time. Standing together is the worst possible arrangement. There is only ever one of him in the world.

All of it optional

One flat config file. A master switch, a pace dial, every event individually, and separate toggles for the three things people actually argue about.

“You shouldn't have done that.”
— the tenth blow

Install

  1. 1

    Get Fabric

    Install the Fabric loader for Minecraft 26.2, then drop Fabric API into your mods folder.

    fabricmc.net ↗
  2. 2

    Drop in the jar

    Put herobrine-1.0.0.jar in your mods folder, beside Fabric API.

  3. 3

    Play normally

    Don't go looking. It starts on its own, and the first hour is meant to feel like nothing is installed.

Joining a server?

Then don't do the three steps above — do this instead. Minecraft can push a resource pack to a joining player and nothing else; it has never been able to install mods, so a server cannot hand you this one. You have to arrive already holding it, at the same version the server is running.

The modpack is that, in one file. Import it into Modrinth App or Prism Launcher and it fetches Fabric Loader, Fabric API and Herobrine at the pinned versions and makes a profile. Launch from that profile, not the vanilla launcher.

Download modpack .mrpack

Updates

Every fix ships as a new release. Watch the repository and GitHub will tell you when one lands — then swap the jar in your mods folder. Your world and your config carry over.
