Download latest · .jar How to install

What it is

Not a quest log. Nothing is marked on your map and nobody tells you what to do — you work it out, the way the myth was always supposed to work.

  1. 1

    Your world starts going wrong. Small things first. You will talk yourself out of most of them.

  2. 2

    Somebody left a trail. Maps, books, places to find. Follow it and it gets worse, and clearer.

  3. 3

    At the end of the trail is a door. It goes to his dimension. It shuts behind you.

  4. 4

    Beat him there and your world comes back. Lose, and you go again — he remembers.

Who you'll meet spoilers

The fight

100 hits. Three levels. He gets bigger every time. Try it — click him.

Herobrine
0 / 100 blows

Click him.

LVL 1

A man

He walks at you and swings. Hit him four times in a row and he vanishes — then he is behind you.

LVL 2

Bigger

He rises into the sky, drops, and the blast throws you. Walls stop working. So does hiding.

LVL 3

A shadow

The light goes out of him for good. Two white eyes in the dark, and ten fireballs at a time.

  • No way home until he is dead.
  • Die and you lose the level, not the whole fight. Your body stays where you fell, holding everything.
  • Bring friends — he gets tougher, but you get hands.
