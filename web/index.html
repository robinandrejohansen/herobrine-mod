The story

  1. 1

    A village went bad. Doors boarded themselves. People went missing. The ones who came back were not the same people.

  2. 2

    One man wrote it down. Addexio. Ten books, hidden in every place Herobrine touched.

  3. 3

    The village fought him. They lost. Almost everyone died. Addexio came home alone.

  4. 4

    Then you show up. Addexio finds you. You follow his books: town, tower, prison, church, last house.

  5. 5

    The last house has a door. It goes to his world. Once you step through, it shuts behind you.

  6. 6

    Kill him, or stay there. Win and the rain stops, the taken are people again, and the sun comes up for the first time in years.

The fight

100 hits. Three levels. Try it — click him.

Herobrine
0 / 100 blows

Click him.

LVL 1

A man

He walks at you and swings. Hit him four times in a row and he vanishes — then he is behind you.

LVL 2

Bigger

He rises into the sky, drops, and the blast throws you. Walls stop working. So does hiding.

LVL 3

A shadow

The light goes out of him for good. Two white eyes in the dark, and ten fireballs at a time.

  • No way home until he is dead.
  • Die and you lose the level, not the whole fight. Your body stays where you fell, holding everything.
  • Bring friends — he gets tougher, but you get hands.
